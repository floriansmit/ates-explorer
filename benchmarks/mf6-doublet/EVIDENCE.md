# Executed first comparison — 7 November 2026

An actual paired calculation was completed for case.json using the unchanged
Explorer thermal kernel at commit 707a767592b1eaf8e5dbb2ab7293ef2a92e0b02d and
MODFLOW 6.7.0 (02/05/2026), FloPy 3.10.0, NumPy 2.3.5, with 1,460 six-hour steps.
Both runs cover 8,760 hours. This is one synthetic numerical benchmark, not field
validation or evidence of accuracy across the application's operating range.

| Metric | Explorer | MODFLOW 6 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Injected warm anomaly | 301,449.60 kWh | Same imposed input |
| Warm recovered anomaly | 202,508.53 kWh | 201,901.65 kWh |
| Warm recovery fraction | 67.1782% | 66.9769% |
| Final signed stored anomaly | -202,508.53 kWh | -201,901.65 kWh |
| Whole-run heat ledger residual | -7.68e-9 kWh | 5.79e-6 kWh |

Active-step production-temperature RMSE: **0.01023 °C**; maximum difference:
**0.02319 °C**. Recovered-energy difference is 606.89 kWh, or 0.2013% of injected
warm energy (0.2997% of Explorer recovered energy). Phase-end full-field RMSE is
0.00835–0.01014 °C. Nonempty 1 K plume masks have IoU 0.9821–1.0; warm-charge area
is 5,500 vs 5,600 m² and centroid separation 0.867 m. No >=0.6 K thermal contamination
of the opposite production well occurs during charging. Consequently this case
**does not test breakthrough timing successfully**; it establishes a censored
no-breakthrough result for that phase. A longer charge or closer doublet is needed
for a separate breakthrough challenge. Recovery starts above threshold in both
models at their first reported recovery sample, hour 4,326.

The negative year-end stored anomaly is physically consistent with retained cold
charging plus warm extraction; it is not a negative recovery efficiency. Signed
anomaly and absolute-temperature energy budgets must not be confused.

## Conservation audit

Explorer's storage is independently recomputed from its final field and compared
with its well/boundary ledger. MODFLOW native CBC terms are integrated over every
timestep: STORAGE-CELLBLK + SOURCE-SINK MIX + CTP gives 5.79e-6 kWh residual, with
maximum instantaneous residual 7.91e-10 kWh/h. Independently recomputed interior
storage differs from negative cumulative STORAGE-CELLBLK by 3.79e-6 kWh. Fixed
ambient ghost cells do not change stored anomaly. The reported cumulative CTP
exchange is -0.000825 kWh; Explorer boundary exchange rounds to zero in this case.

GWF WEL + CHD integrates to -5.90e-5 m³ over the year; maximum instantaneous absolute
imbalance is 1.24e-7 m³/h. Rounded phase-end listing heat discrepancy is 0.00%.
Flow rate discrepancy reaches +/-200% in idle phases because the denominator is
near zero (roughly 1e-7 m³/h numerical boundary flux). It is **not** a 200% pumping
mass loss. For idle phases use an absolute-rate threshold, proposed 1e-6 m³/h,
alongside cumulative-volume closure; do not apply a relative-only gate there.

## What was checked and what remains

Executed: production-kernel run, FloPy deck generation, GWF/GWE solve with normal
termination, timestamp/grid checks, production/energy/plume comparison, cumulative
binary water/heat ledgers and final-temperature storage check. Existing software
suite: 48/48 passing; application build passed. Production code was not modified.

Not executed: spatial/time/domain convergence, independent face-flow comparison,
nonzero dispersion/regional-flow benchmarks, breakthrough challenge, repeated
seasonal cycles, demand dispatch, multi-aquifer cases, vertical/aquitard exchange,
field calibration. Close agreement between two coarse first-order upwind runs
can conceal shared numerical diffusion. The proposed convergence/interpretation
gates in README.md remain necessary before a stronger accuracy claim.

Reusable inputs, logs, exact solver provenance and machine-readable metrics are
in evidence/. Large native binary fields/budgets are generated by the commands
in README.md rather than committed. The evidence includes native listings and the run log in `evidence/native-logs.zip`,
and all generated text model inputs. No MODFLOW installation is bundled; rerunning
requires installing an executable with GWE support. All synthetic physical inputs
needed for this case are supplied.
